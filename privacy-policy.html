Privacy Policy

Universal QR Scanner · Last updated: October 30, 2026

Universal QR Scanner ("the App", "we") is published by Kalukunda and available at https://scanner.kalukunda.com.np/ and through the Microsoft Store. This policy explains what information the App handles and how.

Summary

The App does not require an account, and we do not collect, store, sell, or share your personal information. Scanning and code generation happen on your device.

Information we do not collect

  • We do not ask you to create an account or provide your name, email address, or any other personal details.
  • We do not collect or store the content of the QR codes or barcodes you scan or generate.
  • We do not collect or store the images you scan or the logos you add to generated codes.
  • We do not access your device location. "Location" in the App is only a type of content you can encode into a QR code.
  • We do not use advertising, advertising identifiers, or analytics or tracking tools.

Permissions the App uses

  • Camera: used only for live scanning of QR codes and barcodes. Camera frames are processed on your device and are not recorded, saved, or sent to us. You can stop the camera at any time in the App or revoke permission in your device or browser settings.
  • Files and clipboard images: used only when you choose, drop, or paste an image to scan or to use as a logo. These images are processed locally on your device.

Data stored on your device

The App may keep preferences or cached files on your device (for example, through browser storage and offline caching) so that it can work offline. This data stays on your device, is not sent to us, and can be removed by clearing the App's data or uninstalling it.

Links and scanned content

A scanned code may contain a link, phone number, email address, or other content. If you choose to open a link or take an action from a scanned code, you will be interacting with a third-party website or app that has its own privacy practices. We are not responsible for those third parties. Be careful when opening links from codes you do not trust.

Hosting and network requests

The App is delivered from scanner.kalukunda.com.np. As with any website, the hosting provider may automatically process technical information such as your IP address and browser type when your device downloads or updates the App. We do not use this information to identify you.

Children's privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties, because we do not collect it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page, with the "Last updated" date changed.
